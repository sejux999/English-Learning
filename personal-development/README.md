
# Personal English Development

Welcome to my personal English learning space!

My current English level is A2 (Elementary).

My main goal is to improve my English proficiency to B1 (Intermediate) and beyond.

## Learning Areas

### 1. Vocabulary
Learning new words, meanings, synonyms, and example sentences.

### 2. Grammar
Understanding English grammar rules and sentence structures.

### 3. Reading
Improving reading comprehension through texts and articles.

### 4. Listening
Developing listening skills through conversations and audio materials.

### 5. Speaking
Practicing daily conversations and improving fluency.

### 6. Writing
Developing sentence construction, paragraphs, and writing skills.

## Learning Goals

- Learn 10 new vocabulary words every day.
- Study grammar regularly.
- Practice speaking English.
- Write short English paragraphs.
- Review my progress every week.

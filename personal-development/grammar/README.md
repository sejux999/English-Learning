
# Grammar Learning

Welcome to my English grammar learning section!

My goal is to understand English grammar
and use it correctly in speaking and writing.

## Grammar Topics

### 1. Basic Grammar
- Parts of Speech
- Subject and Verb
- To Be (am, is, are)
- Articles (a, an, the)

### 2. Tenses
- Simple Present Tense
- Present Continuous Tense
- Simple Past Tense
- Simple Future Tense
- Present Perfect Tense

### 3. Sentence Structure
- Positive Sentences
- Negative Sentences
- Yes/No Questions
- WH-Questions

### 4. Other Grammar Topics
- Prepositions
- Conjunctions
- Modal Verbs
- Comparative and Superlative

## Learning Goals

- Understand grammar rules.
- Recognize sentence patterns.
- Identify common grammar mistakes.
- Practice making correct sentences.
